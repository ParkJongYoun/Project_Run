Scene_All3D — rider motion and material revision

Causes confirmed in the project
- The rider clip was sampled only between 0.23 and 0.37 seconds of a 1-second seated animation.
- Mount playback used speed / arbitrary denominator without accounting for source clip cycles.
  Wolf and boar clips are 0.8 seconds and contain TWO gallops; firewolf (0.6 s) and fox (0.5 s) contain ONE.
- All 15 unique character/mount materials used ProjectRun/CharacterSimple, an Unlit pass without scene lighting,
  reflection or ShadowCaster support. The optional local self-shading keyword was also disabled.
- Source body and mount textures are 512x512; hair is 256x256. Mount meshes are roughly 2,400–6,000 triangles.
  Original materials have no assigned normal maps. These are real source-detail limits, not a render-resolution bug.

Changes
- Entire seated cycle, saddle following, delayed spine/chest/head/arm/cape motion and corner lean.
- Animation sampling uses interpolated simulation time; pause freezes the pose and 2x scales presentation time.
- Original-speed ground-contact reference (world metres/sec, including model scale 2.2):
  wolf 25.8; firewolf 15.0; fox 9.3; boar 12.3.
  At a common travel speed of 13 m/s, respective clip rates are approximately 0.50, 0.87, 1.40, 1.06.
- Race-only URP Lit material copies; original tint retained; restrained skin/fur specular and brighter armour.
- Estimated specular/gloss maps for armour atlases, copied textures with mipmaps/trilinear filtering/aniso 4.
  These estimated masks are provisional material separation, not authored PBR maps or new surface detail.
- Baked 128px HDR environment reflection, camera-relative soft portrait fill, actual shadow casting/receiving.
  The fill uses URP rendering layer bit 2, assigned only to racers, so the existing course exposure is preserved.
  Face materials have a small painted-light contribution and omit hair-cast shadows for readable expressions.
- Existing scene SMAA retained. No runtime reflection captures and no new post-processing stack.
- OldAsset originals and shared CharacterSimple shader remain untouched.

Validation
- See Motion/motion-validation.txt for measurements from actual Play Mode (all four runners, pause and 2x).
- See ../simulation-validation.txt for identical finish times at 30/60/120 fps, 1x/2x and skip-to-result.
- Four runners, 15 supported Lit materials, baked reflection connected, no missing bones/materials.
- Compilation and runtime error checks passed. Validation is in the Unity Editor, not target mobile WebGL hardware.
